Exam Prep

Don’t let the exam be the first time you attempt something: without notes, under time pressure, from a blank page, with mixed problem types.

Learning Strategies

Exam prep

Study Like You Will Be Tested

Solve unfamiliar problems without notes.

Practice starting from a blank page.

Show your reasoning.

Mix problem types.

Practice under realistic time constraints.

Identify mistakes—not just correct answers.

Know what resources will/won't be available.

During the Exam

Preview the exam.

Start where you can demonstrate knowledge.

Don't get trapped on one problem, move on and come back.

Read the entire question.

Mark important constraints/units/information.

Show your work.

If stuck, write what you do know and identify what the problem is asking.
